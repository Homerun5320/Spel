| `src/render/quadtree.ts` (M07a) | The terrain quadtree, pure and allocation-free per call: 8 roots (4 x 2, 90 degrees), nodes (level, ix, iy) split while the camera is closer than K times their side (distance = a lower bound to the patch between its lowest and highest terrain; K is `chunkSplitK` for cells of four texels and more and falls geometrically to `chunkDeepK` at 1/8 texel per cell), merge at 1 + `CHUNK_HYSTERESIS` times that; frustum culling by the bounding sphere, horizon culling by the angle.

# Architecture detail: AI

Full module rows moved verbatim from `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (the index keeps one short row per module). Rows keep the old index order.

| Path | Purpose | Public API |
|------|---------|------------|
| `src/ai/memory.ts`, `src/ai/place.ts`, `src/ai/think.ts`, `src/ai/index.ts` | AI v0 (M05 part C, D102 to D104). Pure functions over a `SimView`; they import ONLY `src/sim/public.ts` (ESLint allow-list, structural scan and a TypeScript type test). `AiMemory` (per seat: tribe flag, spawn slot, release tick, thinking phase, retry tick, attempts, `lastAttack` per ownerId; plain data). `pickSpawnCell(view, rng, clearance, anchor, reach)`: a seeded search for a whole capital disc of playable land with clear ground, near the anchor (the human capital). `think(view, memory, rng)`: Spawn (the AI seats wait for the human capital or tick 110, then place their capitals 3 ticks apart, nations first), then once per second per seat (staggered) Attack the weakest neighbour with at least 1.3 times its troops (a nation does this only from tick `AI_WAR_START_TICKS` 2000 (a human seat only from `AI_WAR_HUMAN_START_TICKS` 3600 unless it attacks first), only against a neighbour with `AI_WAR_MIN_CONTACT_LENGTH` cells of contact, and only while it owns less than `AI_WAR_MAX_SHARE_PCT` 15 % of all land; at the cap it calls off its wars with a `CancelAttack`, only for attacks `view.attackRunning` says still run, and answers a neighbour that attacks it; from `AI_EXPAND_MAX_SHARE_PCT` 20 % it also expands no more: tempo pass, D142, D143), else unowned land that touches it, at 50 % of the cap and above; a tribe only expands and stops at 600 cells. `AiMemory.human` flags the human seats (the driver passes them). | `think`, `createAiMemory`, `pickSpawnCell`, `AiMemory` |
| `src/ai/build.ts` (M09 part B) | `buildMove(view, memory, rng)`: the AI's build move (City, Factory per 2 Cities, Port, Post; an Upgrade when it is cheaper than the next City), places sampled in a window around the capital and checked with `validateBuild`; called by `AiDriver` after `think`, nations only. `AiDriver.serialize()` / `restore(blob)` give and take the AI state (memories from `memoryWords`, the random streams, the Spawns in flight) as one blob for M24b. `tests/ai/build.test.ts`. |
| `src/ai/build.ts` districts (Districts-1) | `districtMove` inside `buildMove`: fills free slots in slot order Market, Barracks, Housing, Walls, then upgrades the cheapest; only when rich, from `AI_DISTRICT_MIN_CITIES` Cities, interior Cities, back-off `AI_DISTRICT_RETRY_TICKS` (`AiMemory.districtRetryAt`, `MEMORY_FIXED_WORDS` 6). Tests: `tests/sim/districts.test.ts`, `tests/sim/districtsState.test.ts`, `tests/sim/districtsReport.test.ts`, `tests/ai/districts.test.ts`, `tests/host/districtMirror.test.ts`, `tests/districts.perf.test.ts`. |

## Rules for layers that sit on the terrain (M07a audit; read before M08, M10, M12/M13a, M15a)

1. Precision of props. The terrain material places a vertex relative to the eye (`uCamHi` / `uCamLo`, the chunk's centre as a high and a low part), so it is exact to millimetres at 300 m above the ground. A prop placed from absolute float32 coordinates (an `InstancedMesh` whose `instanceMatrix` holds positions near the 6.4e6 m planet radius, a helicopter, a shadow blob, dust, a missile) has a float32 ulp of 0.5 m there and would swim against the ground as the camera moves. The rule for every such layer: keep the position in float64 on the CPU as an ORIGIN (the centre of the region the layer covers, re-based when the camera has moved more than about 20 km from it) and store only the OFFSETS from that origin in the instance matrices (under 100 km: millimetres). The mesh's own `position` is the origin minus the camera, formed in float64 on the CPU each frame (or the origin is passed as a high / low pair like `uCamHi` / `uCamLo`, with the same two-part subtraction in the vertex shader). A layer that needs a GLSL snippet for it adds it to `src/render/shaders/common.ts` once. Test: the jitter test of `e2e/terrain.test.ts` (a camera shift of 1e-7 degrees changes under 0.2 % of the pixels) is repeated with the layer switched on.
2. Height of things above the ground. The renderer displaces the ground by `HEIGHT_EXAGGERATION` (12) times the 10 km raster; the simulation's altitudes (game metres: `EXAG_sim * meanGround(cell) + AGL`, or occluder + clearance, from `src/sim/rules.ts`) are another scale (`GAMEPLAY_HEIGHT_EXAG`, M11a). A prop drawn at the sim altitude would sit inside the visible mountains (Himalaya: about 40 km against a displaced ground at 60 km). The rule: keep the height ABOVE the ground and re-base it on the visible ground: `renderRadius(x, y) = groundRadius(x, y) + (altGame - EXAG_sim * meanSim(cell))` where `groundRadius` is `HeightField.surfaceRadius` (the displaced ground, also under picking) and the bracket is the sim's height above its own ground in game metres, scaled to render units. `radiusAboveGround(groundRadius, aboveGroundM)` of `src/render/transforms.ts` (M08) states this once; M12a/M13a call it. Test: a helicopter instance is at least the clearance above `groundHeightAt` along a real Alps route, and its horizontal position equals the sim's within 0.5 cell.
3. Budgets. Row 0 of "Budgetar" is pinned (150 000 triangles, 100 draw calls since Fix-UI; it was 30) by `tests/budgets.test.ts`. The terrain, the shell and the stars use 3 draw calls and at most 96 000 + 9 024 triangles in the test tier; measured with all layers about 21 000 triangles and 87 draw calls (Fix-3C). Each new layer gets its own row and allowance in the table and in `tests/budgets.test.ts` in one commit (structures at most 15 000 triangles); if the layers ever need more than the free triangles, lower the test tier's `chunkMax` (150 to 100) in the same commit and regenerate the contact-sheet goldens once. Nobody else edits row 0.
4. Heights that M08 compares: three heights exist at a position (the DATA height, the GROUND height `groundHeightAt` before the x12 exaggeration, the SEEN height `seenHeightAt`); a prop that must stand on the drawn ground uses the seen height, a helicopter's clearance the ground height. Full text in [render](arch/render.md) "Rule 4 in full".
